Why people reach out

Maybe some of this sounds familiar.

Most people don't arrive with a neat explanation. That's fine. Figuring out what's actually going on is part of the work we do together.

  • Your mind won't stop running, and the worry follows you to bed.
  • A relationship feels strained, and you can't tell what to do next.
  • You're facing a big decision or a change you didn't plan for.
  • Everything looks fine from the outside, but you don't feel like yourself.

My approach

Warm, direct, and built around you

Clients often describe me as warm, genuine, and funny. Sessions feel like a real conversation, with structure underneath it. Early on, we'll get clear on what's getting in your way and agree on a plan to address it.

Many clients notice things starting to lift within the first month, and we keep building from there.

Cognitive Behavioral Therapy
Spot how your thoughts shape your feelings, and practice responding to them differently.
Motivational Interviewing
Explore your own reasons for change, at your pace, without pressure.
Strengths-Based
Start from what's already working in your life and build on it.
Interpersonal
Look at how your relationships affect your mood, and the other way around.
Solution-Focused
Keep the work practical and pointed at the change you want to see.
About Natalie

Hi, I'm Natalie.

I'm a Licensed Independent Clinical Social Worker based in Needham. I earned my master's in social work at Boston University and an M.A. in clinical psychology from the American School of Professional Psychology.

I've worked with college students at Penn State, Boston University, and Emerson College, and with adolescents in the juvenile justice system and in intensive residential treatment.

That range taught me to meet people where they are. Whether you're a teenager figuring out who you are or an adult rethinking how things are going, my job is to help you make sense of it and find a way forward that fits your life.

Getting started

What happens after you reach out

  1. A free 15-minute call

    We'll go deeper on what brought you in, what you want to change, and how we'll work on it together.

    Regular online sessions, with check-ins along the way to make sure therapy keeps working for you.
